## Required Tools

You will need cookie sheets, a small saucepan, liquid and dry measuring cups, two bowls, parchment paper and a mixer.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 c. butter
- 4 unsweetened chocolate baking squares
- 3 c. semisweet chocolate chips, **divided**
- 1 1/2 c. all-purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp. baking powder
- 1/2 tsp. salt
- 4 eggs
- 1 1/2 c. sugar
- 2 tsp. vanilla extract
- 2 c. toasted chopped pecans

---

## Baking Instructions

Preheat oven to **350&#176;**

In the saucepan, combine butter, unsweetened chocolate and 1 1/2 cups of the chocolate chips and melt over low heat, stirring constantly; cool.

Next, combine flour, salt and baking powder in a small bowl and set aside.

In a larger bowl, beat the eggs, sugar and vanilla with an electric mixer until combined.
Slowly add in the flour mixture to the egg mixture, beating until fully combined.
Add in the chocolate mixture and beat until blended. Stir in the remaning 1 1/2 c. chocolate chips and the pecans.

Drop dough by tablespoonfuls onto a parchment paper-lined cookie sheets.

> Note: Cookies will spread while baking

Bake in batches at **350&#176;** for **10 minutes**. Cool on the baking sheets for 5 minutes, then remove to cool on wire racks for around 20 minutes.
