## Required Tools

You will need two mixing bowls, a liquid measuring cup, dry measuring cups, measuring spoons, parchment paper and a 10x16 in. pan.

A smaller, 10x13 in. pan will work, if it is around 4-5 inches deep.

---

## Ingredients

- 7 eggs, **separated**
- 1 c. honey
- 1/2 c. oil
- 1/4 c. orange juice
- 1 tsp. baking powder
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 c. coffee, cooled
- Drop of almond extract
- 1 c. sugar
- 2 1/2 c. flour

---

## Baking Instructions

In the first bowl, mix the egg yolks and all the ingredients except the sugar.

In the second bowl, beat the egg whites and sugar into a snow until it forms stiff peaks.

> When making the snow, make sure the mixer beaters or whisk you are using is completely clean and dry, or the snow will not form well.

Gently fold the snow in by hand into bowl #2. Make sure it is fully incorporated.

Line your pan with parchment paper, and trim the edges of the paper so they don't burn.

Pour the batter into the pan, filling it up around 1.5-2 inches deep. If you have excess batter, you can make cupcakes with it.

Bake for **30 minutes** at **365&#176;** farenheit. A toothpick inserted into the center should come out dry.
_If making cupcakes, check them after 20 minutes for doneness_

Let cool for at least one hour, or the cake may lose its fluffiness when cut.
