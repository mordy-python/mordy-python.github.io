Honey Cake

Fri 11 September 2026 by Izzy W

Required Tools

You will need two mixing bowls, a liquid measuring cup, dry measuring cups, measuring spoons, parchment paper and a 10x16 in. pan.

Ingredients

  • 7 eggs, separated
  • 1 c. honey
  • 1/2 c. oil
  • 1/4 c. orange juice
  • 1 tsp. baking powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 c. coffee, cooled
  • Drop of almond extract
  • 1 c. sugar
  • 2 1/2 c. flour

Baking Instructions

In the first bowl, beat the egg whites and sugar into a snow

In the second bowl, mix the egg yolks and the rest of the ingredients.

Gently fold the snow in by hand into bowl #2. Make sure it is fully incorporated.

Line your pan with parchment paper, and trim the edges of the paper so they don't burn.

Bake for 30 minutes at 365° farenheit. A toothpick inserted into the center should come out dry.
